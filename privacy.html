Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 May 2026

Pitchify is a native iOS ear-training app. It is designed to work fully offline and to collect nothing about you.

Data we collect

None. Pitchify does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, location, or analytics. There are no user accounts. There are no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your local preferences (selected instrument, difficulty, streak counters) are saved on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This data never leaves your phone. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Pitchify is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
