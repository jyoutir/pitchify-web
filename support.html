Frequently asked

Does Pitchify need an internet connection?
No. The app works fully offline and never makes a network request.

Does Pitchify use my microphone?
No. It only plays notes through your speakers or headphones.

How do I reset my progress?
Delete and reinstall the app. All local data is removed when you uninstall.

Which instruments are supported?
Multiple instrument timbres are bundled — see the instrument picker in the app.

Privacy
